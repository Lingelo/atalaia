Atalaia — wildfires in Portugal and Spain

Atalaia is a live map of ongoing wildfires in Portugal and Spain, built from the civil protection services' own published data. It is free, requires no account, and is available in Portuguese, Spanish and English.

Where the data comes from

  • Portugal — ANEPC occurrences via fogos.pt: location, status, personnel, vehicles and aircraft deployed.
  • Andalusia — Plan INFOCA, Junta de Andalucía.
  • Catalonia — Bombers de la Generalitat de Catalunya.
  • Castile and León — Junta de Castilla y León, including burned area.
  • Satellite thermal detections — NASA FIRMS (VIIRS), shown as a separate, optional layer.

What it does not cover

There is no national Spanish real-time wildfire service. Coverage is limited to the three autonomous communities listed above: a fire in Galicia, Extremadura or Valencia will not appear on this map. An empty area therefore means “no data”, never “no fire”. The application states this on screen, and reports the status of every service it queries.

Confirmed incidents versus satellite detections

The two are deliberately never mixed. Civil protection incidents are drawn as solid discs and carry a verified status and deployed resources. NASA FIRMS detections are drawn as hollow, ember-coloured rings: they are thermal anomalies measured from orbit, unconfirmed on the ground, and frequently caused by agricultural burning, volcanoes or industrial flares. They are never added into national totals.

How fresh the data is

Incidents are rebuilt roughly every ten minutes and served as static files; the interface displays the age of the data itself, rather than the time you happened to load the page. Satellite detections are refreshed hourly, since the satellites pass over only every few hours.
